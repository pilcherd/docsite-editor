# Build your first collection

Bring your notes, sketches and decisions together in **Maple Notes**, our fictional example app.

## A place for every idea

Start with a small collection. Add a note, give it a clear title and move it along as your idea takes shape.

## Choose your workspace

<Tabs groupId="workspace" aria-label="Workspace type">
<TabItem value="personal" label="Personal" default>

Keep a private journal, collect reading notes or plan your next creative project.

1. Select **New collection**.
2. Name it **Garden journal**.
3. Add your first note.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="team" label="Team">

Give your team a shared place to collect ideas and record decisions.

1. Create a collection named **Workshop ideas**.
2. Invite a teammate.
3. Agree which notes are ready to share.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Give each note a clear status

| Status      | What belongs here             |
| ----------- | ----------------------------- |
| Ideas       | Notes you want to explore     |
| In progress | Work with an agreed next step |
| Ready       | Notes you can share or reuse  |

Read [Collection settings](/collections) to choose a layout, or follow the [Weekly review](/weekly-review) to keep things tidy.

## Try a reusable template

The following command is an illustration for this fictional app:

```sh title="Example command"
maple collection create "Garden journal"
```

:::tip Start small

A few useful notes are easier to maintain than an empty library of folders.

:::
